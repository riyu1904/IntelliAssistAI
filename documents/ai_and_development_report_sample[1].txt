AI and Development: Report Reading Sample
Short, original practice document for IntelliAssist AI
Purpose
This short practice note introduces themes a reader may encounter when reviewing public reports about
artificial intelligence and economic development. It is a test document, not an official World Bank
publication.
Potential opportunities
AI systems may help people analyze information, improve some public and business services, support
learning, and automate repetitive tasks.
The benefits depend on the quality of data, suitable infrastructure, staff skills, responsible implementation,
and whether people can access the resulting services.
Risks and implementation considerations
Risks can include inaccurate outputs, privacy concerns, bias, unequal access, security weaknesses, and
overreliance on automated decisions.
Organizations should evaluate systems before deployment, protect sensitive information, monitor
performance, and ensure that people can review consequential decisions.
Questions to test in IntelliAssist AI
Name two potential opportunities associated with AI.
List three risks that organizations should consider.
Why is human review important for consequential decisions?
Which implementation factors can affect whether AI benefits are realized?
