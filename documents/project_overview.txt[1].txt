Project Name: IntelliAssist AI

Overview:
IntelliAssist AI is an AI-powered document chatbot that
helps users search and understand their documents.

Problem Statement:
Students and businesses often struggle to find relevant
information across PDFs, notes, and other documents.
Manually searching large documents can be time-consuming.

Solution:
IntelliAssist AI allows users to upload documents and ask
questions about their contents. The system retrieves
relevant information and generates context-aware answers.

Key Features:
1. Document upload for PDF, TXT, and DOCX files.
2. AI chatbot powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
3. Semantic search using text embeddings.
4. Automatic document summarization.
5. Sentiment and intent analysis.
6. Source citations in generated answers.
7. Conversation history.
8. Streamlit web interface.

Technology Stack:
Frontend: Streamlit
Backend: Python
AI Framework: LangChain
Embeddings: Hugging Face
Vector Database: FAISS or ChromaDB
Deployment: Streamlit Cloud or Render

Expected Outcome:
A user-friendly application that helps users retrieve
information from their documents quickly and efficiently.
