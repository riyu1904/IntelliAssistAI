Natural Language Processing
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of artificial intelligence concerned with processing and
understanding human language. Common tasks include text classification, sentiment analysis, translation,
summarization, and question answering.
An NLP system may transform text into numerical representations so algorithms can compare, classify, or
process it.
Transformer models
Transformer architectures use attention mechanisms to model relationships between tokens in a
sequence. They are widely used in modern language systems.
Different model designs support different tasks. Encoder-oriented models are often used for understanding
and classification, decoder-oriented models for generation, and encoder-decoder models for tasks such as
translation and summarization.
Embeddings and semantic search
An embedding maps text to a vector of numbers. Texts with related meanings may have vectors that are
close under a suitable similarity measure.
In semantic search, a query is embedded and compared with document-chunk embeddings. The system
retrieves passages that are semantically relevant, even when they do not share every exact keyword.
